Why it exists

Engineering Mathematics spans four semesters and dozens of units, and the best explanations for any given topic are usually scattered across YouTube, old question papers and someone's typed-up notes. Enggii Tea brings those into one organized place — sorted by semester and unit, searchable, and reviewed before they're published.

Curated

Every upload is reviewed before it appears publicly.

Organized

Resources are grouped by semester and unit, not dumped in one feed.

Interactive

Calculators and a graph visualizer back up the reading.
